Features

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Built specifically for faculty who want speed and simplicity. No sign-ups, no cloud, no complexity.

📋

One-Tap Attendance

Mark students Present or Absent with a single tap. Live counts update in real-time as you go through the roster.

🏫

Class-Wise Enrollment

Each class has its own student roster. Enroll students from a shared pool, or add new ones — different classes, different students.

📊

Excel Import

Bulk-import student lists from .xlsx spreadsheets directly into a class. Duplicate enrollment numbers are automatically detected and skipped.

🔒

Offline & Private

All data stored locally on your device. No internet required, no cloud uploads, no tracking. Your data stays yours.

📅

Date-Flexible Recording

Missed recording yesterday? Pick any past date to log attendance with the built-in date picker. Never lose data.

⚡

Mark All in One Click

Mark the entire class Present or Absent instantly, then adjust individual students. Saves minutes every session.
